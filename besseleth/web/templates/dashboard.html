Import from LinkedIn

LinkedIn has no API for pulling your connections into a third-party app — there's no live sync to offer here. What LinkedIn does let you do: export them yourself as a CSV (Settings & Privacy → Data privacy → "Get a copy of your data" → check Connections → request archive; LinkedIn emails a download link, sometimes after a delay). Upload that CSV here for a one-time bulk import — name, email, LinkedIn URL, and current company/title get filled in automatically (it has no education data, so schools still need adding by hand). Safe to re-upload later; already-added contacts are skipped.
